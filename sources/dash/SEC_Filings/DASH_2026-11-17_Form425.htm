Important Additional Information Will Be Filed with the SEC and Where to Find It

In connection with the Proposed Transaction, DoorDash intends to file with the SEC a registration statement on Form S-4 (the “Registration Statement”) to register the shares of DoorDash common stock to be issued in connection with the Proposed Transaction. The Registration Statement will include a proxy statement of Instacart that also constitutes a prospectus of DoorDash (the “proxy statement/prospectus”). Each of DoorDash and Instacart may also file other relevant documents with the SEC regarding the Proposed Transaction. After the Registration Statement has been declared effective by the SEC, a definitive proxy statement/prospectus will be mailed to stockholders of Instacart as of the record date established for voting on the Proposed Transaction. This release is not a substitute for the Registration Statement, the proxy statement/prospectus, or any other document that DoorDash or Instacart may file with the SEC in connection with the Proposed Transaction. INVESTORS AND SECURITY HOLDERS OF DOORDASH AND INSTACART ARE URGED TO READ THE REGISTRATION STATEMENT, INCLUDING THE PROXY STATEMENT/PROSPECTUS AND DOCUMENTS INCORPORATED THEREIN BY REFERENCE, AND ANY OTHER RELEVANT DOCUMENTS THAT ARE FILED OR WILL BE FILED WITH THE SEC, AS WELL AS ANY AMENDMENTS OR SUPPLEMENTS TO THOSE DOCUMENTS, CAREFULLY AND IN THEIR ENTIRETY IF AND WHEN THEY BECOME AVAILABLE BECAUSE THEY WILL CONTAIN IMPORTANT INFORMATION ABOUT DOORDASH, INSTACART, THE PROPOSED TRANSACTION, THE RISKS RELATED THERETO, AND RELATED MATTERS.

Investors and security holders will be able to obtain free copies of the Registration Statement and the proxy statement/prospectus (when available), as each may be amended or supplemented from time to time, and other documents containing important information about DoorDash, Instacart, and the Proposed Transaction, once such documents are filed with the SEC, through the website maintained by the SEC at www.sec.gov.
