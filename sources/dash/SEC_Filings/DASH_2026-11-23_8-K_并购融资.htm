Item 1.01
Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.
 
Upsized Revolving Credit Facility
 
On November 23, 2026, DoorDash, Inc., a Nevada corporation (the “Company”), entered into a Commitment Increase Supplement (the “Commitment Increase Supplement”) to its existing Revolving Credit and Guaranty Agreement, dated as of November 19, 2019 (as previously amended and restated as of August 5, 2026, the “Existing RCF Credit Agreement” and, as amended by the Commitment Increase Supplement, the “RCF Credit Agreement”), in each case, among the Company, as the borrower, the guarantors party thereto from time to time (if any), the lenders and issuing banks party thereto from time to time and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., as administrative agent, providing for, among other things, a senior unsecured revolving credit facility (the “RCF Facility”) that matures on August 5, 2031.  The Commitment Increase Supplement, among other things, increased the total revolving commitments under the Existing RCF Credit Agreement by $1.5 billion, from a total of $2.0 billion to a total of $3.5 billion.  The amounts available under the RCF Credit Agreement are available to the Company to use for working capital and general corporate purposes, including for certain permitted stock repurchases and acquisitions.  The terms of the RCF Credit Agreement and the RCF Facility remain otherwise unchanged.
 
Term Loan Credit Facility
 
In connection with the pending acquisition (the “Acquisition”) by the Company of Maplebear Inc. d.b.a. Instacart, a Delaware corporation (“Instacart”), pursuant to a contemplated merger of Ibiza Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company (“Merger Sub”), with and into Instacart, with Instacart surviving as a subsidiary of the Company, pursuant to the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated as of October 28, 2026 (as amended, restated, amended and restated, supplemented or otherwise modified from time to time, the “Merger Agreement”), among the Company, Merger Sub and Instacart, which was previously disclosed in a Current Report on Form 8-K filed by the Company with the Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”) on October 29, 2026 (the “Signing Form 8-K”), the Company, on November 23, 2026, entered into a senior unsecured delayed draw term loan credit facility in an aggregate principal amount of $1.792 billion (the “Term Loan Facility”), pursuant to a Term Loan Credit and Guaranty Agreement (the “Term Loan Credit Agreement”), among the Company, as the borrower, the guarantors from time to time party thereto (if any), the lenders from time to time party thereto and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A., as administrative agent (the “TL Agent”).  The Term Loan Credit Agreement permits the Company to increase the aggregate amount of commitments under the Term Loan Credit Agreement on or prior to the funding date under the Term Loan Credit Agreement, so long as the aggregate amount of such increase does not cause the aggregate commitments to exceed $2.0 billion, subject to the receipt of additional commitments from existing and/or new lenders and other conditions to be agreed.
 
Subject to the limited conditionality provisions set forth in the Term Loan Credit Agreement, the loans under the Term Loan Credit Agreement are available to be drawn in a single draw until the earliest of (i) five business days after the Outside Date (as defined in the Merger Agreement, subject to certain extensions and limitations set forth in the Term Loan Credit Agreement), (ii) the valid termination of the Merger Agreement in accordance with its terms, (iii) the closing of the Acquisition without funding the loans under the Term Loan Credit Agreement, and (iv) receipt by the TL Agent of written notice from the Company of its election to terminate the commitments under the Term Loan Credit Agreement.  The proceeds of the loans to be extended under the Term Loan Facility are expected to be used to finance, among other things, the consummation of the Acquisition, the refinancing of certain debt of Instacart and the payment of fees, costs and expenses related to the foregoing. The Term Loan Facility will have no required amortization and will mature on the third anniversary after the funding date thereunder.  Loans under the Term Loan Facility that are repaid or prepaid may not be reborrowed.
 
Loans under the Term Loan Facility will bear interest, at the Company’s option, at (i) a base rate equal to the highest of (A) the prime rate, (B) the higher of the federal funds effective rate or a composite overnight bank borrowing rate plus 0.50%, and (C) Term Secured Overnight Financing Rate (“Term SOFR”) for a one-month interest period plus 1.00% or (ii) Term SOFR (based on an interest period of one, three or six months), in each case, plus an initial margin of 1.00%, subject to potential step-downs to 0.875% or 0.750% based on certain of the Company’s credit ratings as specified in the Term Loan Credit Agreement (the “Company’s Applicable Rating”).  The Company is also obligated to pay other customary fees for a credit facility of this size and type, including an arrangement fee, administration fees and a ticking fee.  Such ticking fee will accrue, beginning on February 25, 2027, on the daily aggregate amount of unfunded delayed draw term loan commitments at a rate ranging from 0.08% to 0.10% per annum, based on the Company’s Applicable Rating.  The Company will have the right to prepay the outstanding loans under the Term Loan Facility, in whole or in part, without premium or penalty.
 
The Company’s obligations under the Term Loan Facility are not secured and are not currently guaranteed by any of its subsidiaries.  The Term Loan Credit Agreement contains representations and warranties, affirmative and negative covenants and events of default provisions that are substantially similar to those in the RCF Credit Agreement, subject to customary “certain funds” and limited conditionality provisions governing funding at the closing of the Acquisition.
 
The foregoing summaries of the Commitment Increase Supplement and the Term Loan Credit Agreement are not complete and are qualified in their entirety by reference to the full text of the Existing RCF Credit Agreement, as supplemented by the Commitment Increase Supplement, and the Term Loan Credit Agreement, as applicable. Copies of the Commitment Increase Supplement and the Term Loan Credit Agreement are attached as Exhibit 10.1 and 10.2, respectively, to this Current Report on Form 8-K and are incorporated by reference in their entirety.

Reduction of Bridge Facility Commitments
 
The commitments that the Company obtained for a 364-day senior unsecured bridge term loan facility in the aggregate principal amount of $13.0 billion under that certain commitment letter, dated as of October 28, 2026, which was previously disclosed in the Signing Form 8-K, have been permanently reduced from $13.0 billion to $11.208 billion on November 23, 2026, as a result of the effectiveness of the Term Loan Credit Agreement with aggregate commitments of $1.792 billion.
