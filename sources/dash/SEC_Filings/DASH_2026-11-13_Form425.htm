What They’re Saying: Local Grocers and State Retail Associations Welcome DoorDash and Instacart Joining Forces
Regional and independent grocers say the proposed combination would build on valued partnerships and help them compete with the largest retailers.

Following the announcement of DoorDash’s agreement to acquire Instacart, regional and independent grocery leaders are joining state retail associations from throughout the country to support the proposed combination, pointing to opportunities to strengthen their businesses and better serve their customers.

DoorDash and Instacart share a mission to help local businesses succeed. Combined, the two will be able to provide local grocers and retailers of all sizes the best of both services, giving them access to the full suite of tools they need to keep up with the biggest players. This translates into more savings, higher quality, and more selection and choice for consumers — which leads to more sales for local businesses and more earnings for Dashers and Shoppers. Local and regional grocers and retail associations representing thousands of businesses from around the country are celebrating the deal.

Here’s what they’re saying:

Chad Milam, Chief Administrative Officer, Milam’s Markets (Miami-Dade County, Florida):
 
“Every day, we have to compete against some of the largest retailers in the world. As a family owned and operated business, we’ve appreciated the growth and technology support both Instacart and DoorDash have provided over the years. With DoorDash and Instacart as one company, we’re confident we’ll be able to compete in the Miami grocery market even more effectively.”
 
Northgate Gonzalez Market (Southern California):
 
“As a family business, we’ve always believed in welcoming every guest like family, whether they visit our stores or shop with us online. We look forward to continuing our work with DoorDash and Instacart to bring the flavors and warmth of Northgate Gonzalez Market to the communities we call home.”
 
Trei Campbell, Chief Merchandising Officer, Woodlands Market (Marin County and San Francisco, California):
 
“Woodlands is a California market built around meals and moments that leave you hungry to come back. Our longstanding partnerships with DoorDash and Instacart help us bring that experience to more of our local communities, and we believe bringing the two companies together will help us deliver it even better.”
 
Manuel Sauceda, Chief Information Officer, JONS Fresh Marketplace (Greater Los Angeles Area):
 
“We take pride in bringing our neighborhoods the foods they love and the flavors that remind them of home. We value our partnerships with both DoorDash and Instacart and are excited about this news. We look forward to continued growth together, reaching more customers and better serving the communities that make JONS who we are.”

George Zoitas, CEO, Westside Market (New York City):
 
“As a family-owned and operated business, Westside Market takes pride in providing the neighborhoods we serve with the highest-quality foods and hard-to-find products. We feel that DoorDash and Instacart coming together helps us continue to provide the best experience for our customers.”
 
Josh Wing, Vice President of IT, Coborn's (Minnesota, North Dakota, South Dakota, Wisconsin, Michigan, and Illinois):
 
“As a regional grocer, we compete every day with much larger players, and Instacart has helped us do that with technology and tools we couldn’t build on our own. We’re encouraged by what Instacart’s potential partnership with DoorDash could mean for grocers like us, with more resources and capabilities to help us grow and serve our communities.”

Misty Deskins, Senior Director of Marketing, Food City (Kentucky, Virginia, Tennessee, Georgia, and Alabama):
 
“We’ve been focused on growing our eCommerce business with DoorDash and Instacart for several years and continue to see customer expectations for the digital experience evolve. As we continue to invest in those platforms, we’re excited about DoorDash’s acquisition of Instacart. Together, these valued partners will enable us to keep innovating to exceed customer needs and improve their shopping experience.”

We’re excited about the potential of these two innovative companies coming together and believe their combined technology, resources, and expertise will create even greater opportunities for regional grocers like Hays to grow, compete, and better serve the communities that depend on us.”

 Bruce Nustad, President, Minnesota Retailers Association:
 
“We’ve heard positive feedback from retailers about how DoorDash and Instacart help them reach customers and meet growing expectations for convenience. DoorDash's acquisition of Instacart would seem to build on those strengths and should enable even better service for retailers and their customers. We support opportunities like this to improve delivery, help retailers grow and make it easier for consumers to shop the stores they value.”
