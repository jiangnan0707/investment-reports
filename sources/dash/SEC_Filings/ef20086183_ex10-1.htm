COMMITMENT INCREASE SUPPLEMENT

      

This COMMITMENT INCREASE SUPPLEMENT, dated as of November 23, 2026 (this “Agreement”) is by and among DOORDASH, INC., a Nevada corporation (the “Borrower”), JPMORGAN CHASE BANK, N.A., as Administrative Agent, Société Générale (the “Assuming Lender”) and the other banks, financial institutions and other institutional lenders party hereto, in their capacities as Lenders (each in such capacity, an “Existing Lender”) and Issuing Banks (each in such capacity, an “Existing Issuing Bank”) under the Credit Agreement (as defined below) and as Increase Lenders (each in such capacity, an “Increase Lender”).

 

W I T N E S S E T H

 

WHEREAS, the Borrower, the Existing Lenders, and the Administrative Agent are parties to that certain Revolving Credit and Guaranty Agreement, dated as of November 19, 2019 (as amended and restated as of August 7, 2020, as amended as of October 31, 2022, as further amended and restated as of April 26, 2024, as further amended and restated as of August 5, 2026, and as further amended, modified, supplemented or restated from time to time prior to the date hereof, the “Credit Agreement”);

 

WHEREAS, the Borrower desires to obtain an increase in the aggregate amount of the Commitments under the Credit Agreement by obtaining Commitments from the Assuming Lender and additional Commitments from each Increase Lender;

 

WHEREAS, the Assuming Lender wishes to be a Lender under the Credit Agreement, with Commitments in the amounts set forth opposite the Assuming Lender's name on Exhibit A hereto; and

 

WHEREAS, each Increase Lender wishes to increase its Commitments by the amounts set forth opposite such Increase Lender’s name on Exhibit A hereto.

 

NOW THEREFORE, in consideration of the premises and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt, adequacy and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties hereby agree as follows:

 

SECTION 1. Defined Terms. Capitalized terms used but not otherwise defined herein (including in the preamble and recitals hereto) have the meanings assigned to them in the Credit Agreement.

 

SECTION 2. Commitment Increase Notice. The Borrower hereby gives notice to the Administrative Agent pursuant to Section 2.19(a) of the Credit Agreement that the Borrower requests a Commitment Increase under the Credit Agreement (the “2026 Commitment Increase”) as follows:

 

(a)      The aggregate amount of the 2026 Commitment Increase is $1,500,000,000.

 

(b)     The Assuming Lender and the Increase Lenders will provide a portion of the 2026 Commitment Increase in an amount set forth opposite the name of such Assuming Lender or Increase Lender, as applicable, on Exhibit A hereto.

 

(c)      The 2026 Commitment Increase shall be effective on the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date (as defined below).

 

 

 

SECTION 3. Commitment Increase.

 

(a)           On the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date, (i) the Assuming Lender shall become a party to the Credit Agreement as a Lender having a Commitment in the amount set forth opposite its name on Exhibit A hereto and shall have the rights and obligations of a Lender thereunder, (ii) the Commitment of each Increase Lender shall be increased by the amount set forth opposite such Increase Lender’s name on Exhibit A hereto, (iii) Schedule 2.1 to the Credit Agreement shall be replaced in its entirety with Exhibit B hereto (the “Amended Commitment Schedule”), (iv) each of the Assuming Lender and the Increase Lenders agrees to make Revolving Loans to the Borrower from time to time in accordance with the terms of the Credit Agreement in an aggregate amount not to exceed at any time outstanding the amount set forth opposite its name on the Amended Commitment Schedule, (v) the Issuing Bank Sublimit of each Increase Lender that is an Issuing Bank is as set forth opposite its name on the Amended Commitment Schedule and (vi) each Increase Lender and the Assuming Lender are hereby granted the title in respect of the 2026 Commitment Increase set forth opposite its name on Exhibit A hereto (if any).

 

(b)          The additional Commitments obtained pursuant to the 2026 Commitment Increase are on terms that are identical to the existing Commitments.

 

(c)           On the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date, the Administrative Agent shall record the new Commitments of the Assuming Lender and each Increase Lender in the Register and give prompt notice of the 2026 Commitment Increase to the Borrower and the Lenders (including the Assuming Lender).

 

(d)          On the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date, (i) any Loans outstanding on such date shall be reallocated among the Lenders (with Lenders making any required payments to each other) to the extent necessary to keep the outstanding Loans ratable with any revised pro rata shares of such Lenders (including the Assuming Lender) and (ii) the participation interests of the Lenders in the then outstanding Letters of Credit shall automatically be adjusted to reflect, and each Lender (including the Assuming Lender) shall have a participation in each such Letter of Credit equal to, the Lenders’ respective Applicable Percentage of the aggregate amount available to be drawn under such Letter of Credit after giving effect to such increase. Each Lender party hereto hereby waives any payment owed to such Lender under Section 2.15 of the Credit Agreement in connection with the allocations and adjustments set forth in this clause (d).

 

(e)           The Assuming Lender (a) represents and warrants that (i) it has full power and authority, and has taken all action necessary, to execute and deliver this Agreement and to consummate the transactions contemplated hereby to become a Lender under the Credit Agreement and under applicable law, (ii) it satisfies the requirements, if any, specified in the Credit Agreement that are required to be satisfied by it in order to become a Lender, (iii) from and after the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date, it shall be bound by the provisions of the Credit Agreement as a Lender thereunder and, to the extent of its Commitment set forth in the Amended Commitment Schedule, shall have the obligations of a Lender thereunder, (iv) it has received and/or had the opportunity to review a copy of the Credit Agreement to the extent it has in its sole discretion deemed necessary, together with copies of the most recent financial statements delivered pursuant to Sections 5.1(a) and 5.1(b) thereof, as applicable, and such other documents and information as it has in its sole discretion deemed appropriate to make its own credit analysis and decision to enter into this Agreement and to make its Commitment on the basis of which it has made such analysis and decision independently and without reliance on the Administrative Agent or any other Lender or any of their respective Related Parties, (v) has provided to the Borrower and the Administrative Agent, together with its signature hereto, any U.S. Internal Revenue Service forms required under Section 2.16 of the Credit Agreement and (vi) has provided its Administrative Questionnaire to the Administrative Agent; (b) agrees that it will, independently and without reliance on the Administrative Agent or any other Lender or any of the Related Parties of any of the foregoing, and based on such documents and information as it shall deem appropriate at the time, continue to make its own credit decisions in taking or not taking action under the Loan Documents; (c) appoints and authorizes the Administrative Agent to take such action as agent on its behalf and to exercise such powers under the Credit Agreement and the other Loan Documents as are delegated to or otherwise conferred upon the Administrative Agent by the terms thereof, together with such powers as are reasonably incidental thereto; and (d) agrees that it will perform in accordance with their terms all of the obligations which by the terms of the Loan Documents are required to be performed by it as a Lender. Without limiting the foregoing, the Assuming Lender represents and warrants, and agrees to, each of the matters set forth in Section 9.4 of the Credit Agreement, including that the Loan Documents set out the terms of a commercial lending facility.

(f)           The Assuming Lender’s address for notices for the purposes of the Credit Agreement is as set forth in its Administrative Questionnaire.

 

(g)          Each of the Administrative Agent, each Existing Issuing Bank (collectively constituting all of the Issuing Banks) and the Borrower hereby approves the addition of the Assuming Lender as a new Lender under the Credit Agreement, to the extent any such approval is required pursuant to the terms of the Credit Agreement.

 

(h)          Each of the Administrative Agent and the Existing Lenders (collectively constituting the Required Lenders) hereby waives the three Business Day advance notice requirement set forth in Section 2.19(a) of the Credit Agreement with respect to the 2026 Commitment Increase and each party hereto agrees that the 2026 Commitment Increase shall become effective on the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date.

 

SECTION 4. Conditions to Effectiveness. This Agreement and the agreements of each party hereto set forth herein shall become effective on the first date on which the following conditions have been satisfied (or waived by each of the parties hereto) (such date, the “2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date”):

 

(a)           the Administrative Agent (or its counsel) shall have received the following, each of which shall be originals or .pdf copies or other facsimiles:

 

(i)           executed counterparts of this Agreement properly executed on behalf of each party hereto;

 

(ii)          a certificate from the secretary or other Responsible Officer of each Loan Party, attaching: (A) a customary certificate of good standing from the secretary of state of the state of organization of such Loan Party; (B) resolutions or other action of the Board of Directors of such Loan Party approving this Agreement, the 2026 Commitment Increase and the related transactions; (C) an incumbency certificate and/or other certificate of a Responsible Officer of such Loan Party evidencing the identity, authority and capacity of each Responsible Officer thereof authorized to act as a Responsible Officer in connection with this Agreement and the other Loan Documents; and (D) such other documents and certificates as the Administrative Agent or its counsel may reasonably request relating to the organization, existence and good standing of such Loan Party; and

 

(iii)        a customary opinion from (A) Latham & Watkins LLP, counsel to the Borrower, and (B) Brownstein Hyatt Farber Schreck, LLP, special Nevada counsel to the Borrower, in each case, addressed to the Administrative Agent and the Lenders (including the Assuming Lender) and dated as of the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date;

 

(b)          no Default or Event of Default shall have occurred and be continuing or will result from the 2026 Commitment Increase;

 

(c)          the representations and warranties set forth in Article III of the Credit Agreement and in the other Loan Documents shall be true and correct in all material respects (other than to the extent qualified by materiality or “Material Adverse Effect”, in which case, such representations and warranties shall be true and correct in all respects) immediately prior to, and after giving effect to, the 2026 Commitment Increase as if made on and as of such date (or, if any such representation or warranty is expressly stated to have been made as of a specific date, as of such specific date);

 

3

 

(d)          the Administrative Agent shall have received a certificate of a duly authorized officer of the Borrower stating that the conditions set forth in clauses (b) and (c) of this Section 4 and all other conditions with respect to the 2026 Commitment Increase under Section 2.19 of the Credit Agreement have been satisfied;

 

(e)           (i) the Administrative Agent shall have received, at least three Business Days prior to the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date, all documentation and other information regarding the Borrower requested in connection with applicable “know your customer” and anti-money laundering rules and regulations, including the Patriot Act, to the extent requested in writing of the Borrower at least ten Business Days prior to the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date and (ii) to the extent the Borrower qualifies as a “legal entity customer” under the Beneficial Ownership Regulation, the Assuming Lender or any Increase Lender that has requested, in a written notice to the Borrower at least three days prior to the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date, a Beneficial Ownership Certification in relation to the Borrower shall have received such Beneficial Ownership Certification; and

 

(f)           the Borrower shall pay all fees and other amounts due and payable to the Administrative Agent and the Lenders (including the Assuming Lender) on or prior to the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date (including any fees payable pursuant to that certain fee letter, dated as of November 9, 2026, by and between the Borrower and JPMCB, delivered in connection with the 2026 Commitment Increase) in connection with the 2026 Commitment Increase and all reasonable, documented, and invoiced out-of-pocket expenses (including the reasonable, documented, and invoiced out-of-pocket fees and expenses of counsel to the Administrative Agent) required to be paid by the Borrower under Section 10.3 of the Credit Agreement in connection with the 2026 Commitment Increase for which the Administrative Agent has submitted a reasonably detailed invoice to the Borrower at least two Business Days prior to the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date.

 

SECTION 5. Effect of this Agreement; No Novation.

 

(a)           Except as expressly set forth herein, this Agreement shall not by implication or otherwise limit, impair, constitute a waiver of, or otherwise affect the rights and remedies of the Administrative Agent, the Issuing Banks, or the Lenders under the existing Credit Agreement or any other Loan Document, and shall not alter, modify, amend or in any way affect any of the terms, conditions, obligations, covenants or agreements contained in the existing Credit Agreement or any other Loan Document, all of which shall continue in full force and effect in accordance with the provisions thereof.

 

(b)          Nothing herein shall be deemed to entitle any Loan Party to a consent to, or a waiver, amendment, modification or other change of, any of the terms, conditions, obligations, covenants or agreements contained in the Credit Agreement or any other Loan Document in similar or different circumstances.

 

(c)           On and after the 2026 Commitment Increase Effective Date, each reference in the Credit Agreement to “this Agreement”, “hereunder”, “hereof”, “herein”, or words of like import, as used in the Credit Agreement, and the term “Credit Agreement”, as used in any Loan Document, shall refer to the Credit Agreement as modified by this Agreement.

 

(d)          Nothing herein contained shall be construed as a substitution or novation of the Obligations outstanding under the Credit Agreement. Nothing expressed or implied in this Agreement, the Credit Agreement or any other document contemplated hereby or thereby shall be construed as a release or other discharge of the Borrower under the Credit Agreement or any Loan Party under any Loan Document from any of its obligations and liabilities thereunder.

 

4

 

SECTION 6. Reaffirmation. Each of the Loan Parties hereby reaffirms its obligations under the Credit Agreement and each other Loan Document to which it is a party, in each case as modified by this Agreement.

 

SECTION 7. Interpretation. This Agreement shall constitute a “Loan Document” for all purposes of the Credit Agreement and the other Loan Documents.

 

SECTION 8. Miscellaneous. Sections 10.2, 10.3, 10.6, 10.9, 10.10, 10.11 and 10.15 of the Credit Agreement shall apply mutatis mutandis to this Agreement as if set forth herein.
