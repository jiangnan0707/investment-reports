             This slide shows the quarterly financial summary. I will mainly refer to the figures in
             the blue box.
             In the second quarter, with a backdrop of active market conditions, we generated net
             sales of 935.0 billion yen, 27.7% increase quarter over quarter, hitting a record high.
             Gross profit was 447.3 billion yen, 30.5% rise from the previous quarter. Gross profit
             margin was 47.8%, 1.0-percentage-point increase quarter over quarter, as fixed costs
             to sales ratio dropped along with the increase of net sales.
             Operating income was 295.3 billion yen, 39.7% increase from the previous quarter.
             Operating profit margin was 31.6%, increasing by 2.7 percentage points quarter over
             quarter.
             Income before income taxes grew by 35.6% to 292.2 billion yen. Net income
             attributable to owners of parent was 212.5 billion yen, 29.4% increase quarter over
             quarter.
             Capital expenditures in the second quarter were 32.3 billion yen.

             This slide shows the balance sheet.
             Total assets were 3 trillion 177.4 billion yen.
             Cash and cash equivalents were 579.7 billion yen, increasing by 170.1 billion yen
             from the previous quarter.
             Notes and accounts receivable were 619.0 billion yen, increasing by 75.0 billion yen
             quarter over quarter.
             Inventories were 747.4 billion yen, declining by 4.0 billion yen quarter over quarter. As
             in the previous quarter, inventory turnover showed improvement.
             Tangible assets were 623.8 billion yen, increasing by 13.4 billion yen from the
             previous quarter.
             Investments and other assets were 458.8 billion yen, declining by 44.4 billion yen from
             the previous quarter, mainly due to fair market valuation of investment securities.

             For the liabilities and net assets shown on the right-hand side, liabilities were 982.8
             billion yen, increasing by 170.3 billion yen quarter over quarter, because of increased
             income taxes payable and provision for employees' bonus, both of which were
             coupled with our financial performance.
             Net assets were 2 trillion 194.6 billion yen, rising by 51.7 billion yen quarter over
             quarter.

             Let me start with the business environment.
             For WFE market, reflecting a very strong demand at present, we have revised the
             guidance we announced at the end of July, and now its size is expected to be around
             160B dollars in CY2026 and more than 210B dollars in CY2027.
             In both years, around 30% year-over-year growth is expected.
             The AI evolution is further expanding growth opportunities of the entire semiconductor
             market.
             In addition to generative AI, along with spread of agentic AI, AI comes to
             autonomously implement multiple tasks, and accordingly the number of tokens is
             significantly increasing.
             As a result, GPU is required to feature higher computing capability, and HBM needs to
             feature more capacity and broader bandwidth to support Key Value cache.
             On top of that, demand for wide range of devices is emerging, including CPU for pre-
             processing and control, DRAM supporting long context, and NAND & SSD storing
             models, data and inference logs.
             In the past, computing performance enhancement was driven by processing power of
             logic devices such as CPU and GPU.
             But in the emerging AI era, the entire system performance is heavily dependent on not
             only how powerful processing is, but also how fast large amount of data can be stored
             and fed to GPUs.
             Accordingly, roles of memory devices such as DRAM and HBM are drastically
             changing. Memory devices are now evolving from a “storage” function to a central key
             component to support AI performance.

             “More tokens, More semiconductors.”

             As AI becomes more sophisticated, memory capacity and bandwidth as well as basic
             computing power increase further, which requires more semiconductor devices and
             more advanced technologies. This will lead to further growth of WFE market.
