EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT AND INDEMNIFICATION AGREEMENT
This Expense Reimbursement and Indemnification Agreement (this “Agreement”), dated as of November 20, 2026, is entered into by and between Anthropic, PBC, a Delaware public benefit corporation (the “Company”), on the one hand, and Anthropic Founders, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (together with any successor thereto, including any entity resulting from a Reorganization (as defined in the Restated Certificate (as defined below)), the “Founder LLC”), on the other hand. The Company and the Founder LLC (the “Indemnitee Party”) are each referred to herein individually as a “Party” and, collectively, as the “Parties.”
RECITALS
WHEREAS, in connection with the initial public offering of the Company’s Class A Common Stock (the “Public Offering”), the Company, the Founder LLC and the Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust, a Delaware noncharitable purpose trust (the “Trust”), have entered into that certain Stockholders’ Agreement, effective as of the IPO Closing Date (as amended or otherwise modified from time to time, the “Stockholders’ Agreement”);
WHEREAS, Section 3.9 of the Stockholders’ Agreement provides for the advancement, direct payment and reimbursement by the Company of the Expenses (as defined below) of the Founder LLC incurred in connection with Covered Matters (as defined below);
WHEREAS, Section 3.10 of the Stockholders’ Agreement requires the Company, in connection with the Public Offering, to execute and deliver an indemnification agreement with the Founder LLC providing for the indemnification of the Founder LLC and its members, among others;
WHEREAS, each member of the Founder LLC has entered into an indemnification agreement with the Company in satisfaction of Section 3.10 of the Stockholders’ Agreement; and
WHEREAS, this Agreement constitutes the indemnification agreement contemplated by Section 3.10 of the Stockholders’ Agreement with respect to the Founder LLC.
AGREEMENT
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the foregoing recitals and the covenants and conditions herein set forth, the Parties hereto agree as follows:
Section 1.Advancement and Reimbursement of Expenses.
(a)As between the Company, on the one hand, and the Founder LLC, on the other hand, the Company shall, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, be solely responsible for and shall bear all Expenses (as defined below) incurred in connection with a Covered Matter (as defined below). The Company shall, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, promptly upon, and in any event within thirty (30) days after, the written request of the Founder LLC, advance to the Founder LLC, or pay directly on its behalf, all reasonable and documented out-of-pocket obligations, costs, fees and expenses, including the reasonable fees and expenses


of counsel (collectively, the “Expenses”) incurred in connection with (i) the administration and maintenance of the Founder LLC, including tax returns and filings (including, but not limited to, FBAR filings and other regulatory filings) and any taxes, fees or similar charges incurred by the Founder LLC; (ii) the ownership, voting or disposition of the Shares (as defined in the Stockholders’ Agreement), including the appraisal thereof; (iii) the exercise or performance of, or the failure to exercise or perform, any right, power, duty or obligation under the Stockholders’ Agreement, the Company’s Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation, duly adopted and filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware in connection with the Public Offering (as it may be amended or otherwise modified from time to time, the “Restated Certificate”), the Company’s bylaws (as amended from time to time, the “Bylaws”), the Limited Liability Company Agreement of the Founder LLC, dated as of November 20, 2026 (as amended or restated from time to time, the “Founder LLC Agreement”), the Amended and Restated Trust Agreement of the Trust, in effect as of the time of the Public Offering (as it may be amended or otherwise modified from time to time, the “Trust Agreement”) or the Amended and Restated Resource and Information Agreement, dated as of November 20, 2026, by and between the Company and the Trust (as amended or restated from time to time, the “Resource Agreement”); (iv) the dissolution, winding up or termination of, or any post-termination liability in respect of, the Founder LLC; (v) the formation, structuring, negotiation, documentation, execution, delivery or amendment of the Stockholders’ Agreement, the Restated Certificate, the Bylaws, the Founder LLC Agreement, the Trust Agreement, the Resource Agreement, the Class E Common Stock Documentation (as defined in the Stockholders’ Agreement) or this Agreement, or any Reorganization; (vi) the Public Offering (including any actual or alleged statement or omission in the registration statement, any prospectus (including the founders’ letter included therein) or any other offering materials or communications relating thereto, regardless of the capacity in which any Founder LLC Indemnitee acted or is alleged to have acted) or (vii) any Proceeding (as defined below) to establish, enforce or interpret any right to indemnification or advancement of expenses of any Founder LLC Indemnitee under this Agreement, the Stockholders’ Agreement, the Restated Certificate, the Bylaws, any indemnification agreement between the Company and any Founder LLC Indemnitee or any other agreement with the Company (each of clauses (i) through (vii), a “Covered Matter”). Without limiting the foregoing, Expenses shall include all reasonable and documented (A) fees and expenses of experts, witnesses, accountants, consultants and appraisers, (B) electronic discovery and document production costs, (C) travel and meeting costs of the members of the Founder LLC incurred in connection with any Covered Matter and (D) costs and expenses incurred in investigating, defending, being a witness or deponent in or otherwise participating in any Proceeding arising out of or relating to any Covered Matter, which shall in all cases be advanced, paid or reimbursed under this Section 1. “Proceeding” means any threatened, pending or completed action, suit, arbitration, mediation, alternate dispute resolution mechanism, claim or threatened claim, cause of action, investigation, inquiry, hearing or other proceeding. To the extent any such Expense has already been paid by the Founder LLC, the Company shall instead reimburse the Founder LLC within thirty (30) days following a written request therefor.
(b)The Founder LLC shall not be required to incur any liability, or to advance any funds, in respect of any Covered Matter other than from amounts provided by the Company.

(c)Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, advancement or reimbursement of the Expenses will not be required in respect of amounts (i) determined by a final non-appealable judgment or order of a court of competent jurisdiction (A) not to have been reasonably related to a Covered Matter or (B) to have resulted from the actual fraud or willful misconduct of the Founder LLC or the applicable Founder LLC Indemnitee; provided that the actual fraud or willful misconduct of any Founder LLC Indemnitee shall not be imputed to any other Founder LLC Indemnitee (other than to the Founder LLC, with respect to any authorized act or omission by or on behalf of the Founder LLC); (ii) incurred in connection with any Proceeding (or any part thereof) initiated by the Founder LLC or any other Founder LLC Indemnitee against the Company, any of its subsidiaries or any of their respective directors or officers (in their capacities as such), unless (A) the Company’s board of directors authorized the Proceeding (or the relevant part thereof) prior to its initiation, (B) the Company provides the indemnification, in its sole discretion, or (C) the Proceeding (or the relevant part thereof) constitutes a Covered Matter pursuant to clause (vii) of Section 1(a); or (iii) constituting any judgment, award, fine, penalty, amount paid in settlement or award of fees or costs, in each case payable to the Company or any of its subsidiaries by the Founder LLC or any other Founder LLC Indemnitee.
Section 2.Indemnification of Founder LLC Indemnitees; Advancement of Expenses.
(a)The Company agrees to indemnify, defend, exonerate and hold harmless, to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Founder LLC and each of the Founder LLC’s former, current and future affiliates, officers, directors, managers, trustees, partners, members, stockholders, employees, agents and representatives, each Estate Successor (as defined in the Founder LLC Agreement), in the case of any member of the Founder LLC, each Family Member (as defined in the Founder LLC Agreement) thereof, each Company Designated Official (as defined in the Founder LLC Agreement), and each of the Covered Persons (as defined in the Founder LLC Agreement) (collectively, the “Founder LLC Indemnitees”) from and against any and all threatened, pending or completed Proceedings, in each case whether a Founder LLC Indemnitee is involved as a party, witness, deponent or otherwise, and any and all liabilities, losses, damages, judgments, fines and penalties (in each case, to the extent indemnification thereof is permitted by applicable law), amounts paid in settlement, reasonable and documented costs and expenses (including, without limitation, reasonable and documented attorneys’, accountants’, experts’ and consultants’ fees, expenses and disbursements) incurred by the Founder LLC Indemnitees or any of them before, on or after the date of this Agreement, arising out of or in any way relating to any Covered Matter (the “Indemnified Liabilities”); provided that the foregoing indemnification rights will not be available to a Founder LLC Indemnitee to the extent that a court of competent jurisdiction determines by final non-appealable judgment or order that such Indemnified Liabilities arose on account of such Founder LLC Indemnitee’s actual fraud or willful misconduct (which shall not be imputed to any other Founder LLC Indemnitee (other than to the Founder LLC, with respect to any authorized act or omission by or on behalf of the Founder LLC)); and provided, further, that if and to the extent that the foregoing indemnification rights may be unavailable or unenforceable for any reason (other than the actual fraud or willful misconduct of the applicable Founder LLC Indemnitee), the Company hereby agrees to make the maximum contribution to the payment and satisfaction of each of the

Indemnified Liabilities which is permissible under applicable law. The Company shall be obligated to pay in cash promptly, and in any event within thirty (30) days after written request therefor, all expenses and other costs incident to any actual or threatened Proceeding, including those incurred in defending any civil or criminal action arising out of or relating to any event or circumstance to which this Section 2 shall apply. Any payment under this Agreement to any Founder LLC Indemnitee shall be made upon receipt of an undertaking by or on behalf of such Founder LLC Indemnitee to repay such amount if it shall ultimately be determined by a final non-appealable judgment or order of a court of competent jurisdiction that such Founder LLC Indemnitee was not entitled to such payment under this Agreement, which undertaking shall be unsecured and interest-free and shall be accepted without reference to such Founder LLC Indemnitee’s financial ability to make repayment.
(b)The Company will reimburse each Founder LLC Indemnitee, within thirty (30) days after written request therefor, for all reasonable and documented costs and expenses (including reasonable and documented attorneys’ and experts’ fees and expenses and any other litigation-related expenses) as they are incurred in connection with investigating, preparing, pursuing, defending or assisting in the defense of any Proceeding for which the Founder LLC Indemnitee would be entitled to indemnification under the terms of Section 2(a), or any claim, action or proceeding arising therefrom, whether such Founder LLC Indemnitee is involved as a party, witness, deponent or otherwise. The Company agrees that it will not, without the prior written consent of the Founder LLC Indemnitee, settle, compromise or consent to the entry of any judgment in any pending or threatened Proceeding relating to the matters contemplated hereby (if any Founder LLC Indemnitee is a party thereto or has been threatened to be made a party thereto) unless such settlement, compromise or consent includes an unconditional release of the Founder LLC Indemnitee from all liability, without future obligation or prohibition on the part of the Founder LLC Indemnitee, arising or that may arise out of such Proceeding, and does not contain an admission of guilt or liability on the part of the Founder LLC Indemnitee.
(c)Notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, the Company shall not be liable to any Founder LLC Indemnitee in respect of any Indemnified Liabilities (or any related costs and expenses) to the extent the same are determined, by a final non-appealable judgment or order of a court of competent jurisdiction, to have resulted from the actual fraud or willful misconduct of such Founder LLC Indemnitee, which shall not be imputed to any other Founder LLC Indemnitee (other than to the Founder LLC, with respect to any authorized act or omission by or on behalf of the Founder LLC). An adverse judgment or plea of nolo contendere shall not, of itself, create a presumption that any Founder LLC Indemnitee committed actual fraud or willful misconduct. Without limiting the foregoing, and notwithstanding anything to the contrary in this Agreement, the Company shall not be obligated to indemnify, advance or reimburse any amounts to, or make any contribution on behalf of, any Founder LLC Indemnitee in respect of any amounts of the type described in Section 1(c)(ii) or Section 1(c)(iii) (other than costs and expenses incurred in any Proceeding to establish or enforce any right to indemnification or advancement under this Section 2).
(d)For purposes of this Agreement, (i) no act or omission shall constitute “willful misconduct” unless done or omitted not in good faith and with actual knowledge that such act or
4

omission was wrongful; (ii) any act or omission based upon authority given pursuant to a resolution duly adopted by the Company’s board of directors (or a committee thereof), or upon the advice of counsel, shall be conclusively presumed to have been done or omitted in good faith; (iii) “actual fraud” means actual and intentional common law fraud under Delaware law, excluding for the avoidance of doubt any claims based on negligence, recklessness or constructive knowledge; and (iv) actual fraud or willful misconduct shall be established only by clear and convincing evidence.
(e)In any claim, action or proceeding to establish, enforce or interpret any right to indemnification or advancement of expenses hereunder, each Founder LLC Indemnitee shall be presumed to be entitled thereto, and the Company shall bear the burden of proving, by clear and convincing evidence, that such Founder LLC Indemnitee is not so entitled.
(f)The rights of any Founder LLC Indemnitee to indemnification hereunder will be in addition to any other rights any such person may have under the Stockholders’ Agreement (including Section 3.9 thereof) or any other agreement or instrument to which such Founder LLC Indemnitee is or becomes a party or is or otherwise becomes a beneficiary or under applicable law or regulation. The Company hereby acknowledges that each Founder LLC Indemnitee may have certain rights to indemnification, advancement of expenses and/or insurance provided by one or more persons or entities with whom or which such Founder LLC Indemnitee may be associated (including, without limitation, any other Founder LLC Indemnitee). The Company hereby acknowledges and agrees that (i) the Company shall be the indemnitor of first resort with respect to any Indemnified Liability, (ii) the Company shall be primarily liable for all Indemnified Liabilities and any indemnification afforded to any Founder LLC Indemnitee in respect of any Indemnified Liabilities, whether created by law, organizational or constituent documents, contract (including this Agreement) or otherwise, (iii) any obligation of any such other person or entity to indemnify such Founder LLC Indemnitee and/or advance expenses to such Founder LLC Indemnitee in respect of any Proceeding shall be secondary to the obligations of the Company hereunder, (iv) the Company shall be required to indemnify each Founder LLC Indemnitee and advance expenses to each Founder LLC Indemnitee hereunder to the fullest extent provided herein without regard to any rights such Founder LLC Indemnitee may have against any such other person or entity or insurer of any such person or entity and (v) the Company (on behalf of itself and its insurers) irrevocably waives, relinquishes and releases any such other person or entity from any claim of contribution, subrogation or any other recovery of any kind in respect of amounts paid by the Company hereunder. In the event any such other person or entity or its insurers advance or extinguish any liability or loss which is the subject of any Indemnified Liability owed by the Company, the payor shall have a right of subrogation against the Company or its insurer or insurers for all amounts so paid which would otherwise be payable by the Company or its insurer or insurers under this Agreement. In no event will payment of an Indemnified Liability under this Agreement by any such other person or entity or its insurers affect the obligations of the Company hereunder or shift primary liability for any Indemnified Liability to any other person or entity. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the Company shall not be liable under this Agreement to make any payment to or on behalf of any Founder LLC Indemnitee to the extent such Founder LLC Indemnitee has actually received

payment of such amount under any other indemnification agreement with the Company, the Restated Certificate, the Bylaws or any insurance policy maintained by the Company.
(g)The indemnity rights provided to the Founder LLC Indemnitees under this Agreement are cumulative with, and do not supersede, any other indemnification rights such Founder LLC Indemnitees would have at common law or under any other agreement or arrangement and shall remain in full force and effect following any termination of this Agreement.
(h)The Company shall at all times maintain, or cause to be available, funds sufficient to satisfy its obligations under this Agreement as and when due, and shall not take any action that would reasonably be expected to impair its ability to timely satisfy such obligations.
(i)Each Founder LLC Indemnitee shall have the right to control the defense of any Proceeding against such Founder LLC Indemnitee with counsel of its choosing, and the reasonable fees and expenses of such counsel shall constitute Indemnified Liabilities. If the Company assumes the defense of any such Proceeding with the consent of the applicable Founder LLC Indemnitee, such Founder LLC Indemnitee may nonetheless retain separate counsel at the Company’s expense if (i) such Founder LLC Indemnitee reasonably concludes that a conflict of interest may exist between the Company and such Founder LLC Indemnitee in the conduct of such defense or (ii) the Company fails to employ counsel reasonably satisfactory to such Founder LLC Indemnitee. The Company shall promptly notify the Indemnitee Party in writing of any threatened or pending Proceeding of which the Company becomes aware that may give rise to a Covered Matter or an Indemnified Liability, and shall promptly provide copies of all material correspondence and pleadings relating thereto.
(j)Except as expressly provided in Sections 1(c) and 2(c), the obligations of the Company under this Agreement are absolute and unconditional and shall not be subject to any set-off, counterclaim, recoupment, defense or other right that the Company or any of its subsidiaries may have against the Founder LLC or any Founder LLC Indemnitee. To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, the Company irrevocably waives, and agrees not to assert, any claim or defense that this Agreement or any provision hereof is invalid, unenforceable, contrary to public policy or not binding on the Company. The obligations of the Company hereunder shall not be released, discharged, impaired or otherwise affected by any bankruptcy, insolvency, reorganization or similar proceeding of the Company, any change of control of the Company or any change in the composition of the Company’s board of directors.
Section 3.Taxes. Any and all payments required to be made to the Founder LLC or any other Founder LLC Indemnitee (each, a “Recipient”) hereunder shall be made free and clear of, and without deduction for, any federal, state, local, foreign or other taxes, duties and assessments in the nature of a tax imposed by a governmental entity or tax authority (“Taxes”); provided, however, that, if the Company shall be required by applicable law to deduct any Taxes from any payment hereunder, (i) such payment shall be increased as necessary so that, after making all required deductions (including deductions applicable to additional amounts payable under this Section 3), the Recipient receives payments equal to the amount it would have received had no such deductions been made, (ii) the Company shall make such deductions and

Section 4.Term and Termination.
(a)Term. This Agreement will be effective from the date hereof and will remain in effect until terminated pursuant to Section 4(b). The date on which this Agreement is so terminated is referred to herein as the “Termination Date.”
(b)Termination of Agreement. This Agreement may be terminated at any time by the mutual written consent of the Indemnitee Party and the Company. For the avoidance of doubt, the termination of the Stockholders’ Agreement shall not, in and of itself, terminate this Agreement. Subject to Section 4(c), this Agreement shall automatically terminate upon the later to occur of (i) the Class F Sunset Time (as defined in the Restated Certificate) and (ii) the completion of the winding up of the Founder LLC and the filing of its certificate of cancellation with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware.
(c)Effect of Termination. Upon the Termination Date, all rights and obligations of each of the Company and the Founder LLC Indemnitees shall immediately cease and terminate, and no Party shall have any further obligation to the other Party with respect to this Agreement, except that (i) the rights and obligations in Section 1, Section 2, Section 3, Section 9 and Section 12 of this Agreement shall survive termination of this Agreement, and shall continue, with respect to any Covered Matter or any Proceeding arising out of any act, omission or circumstance occurring on or prior to the Termination Date (including any Covered Matter relating to the dissolution, winding up or termination of, or any post-termination liability in respect of, the Founder LLC); and (ii) the termination of this Agreement will not relieve any Party from liability for any breach of this Agreement on or prior to the Termination Date.
Section 5.Amendments and Waivers. No amendment or waiver of any provision of this Agreement, or consent to any departure by either Party from any such provision, shall be effective unless the same shall be in writing and signed by each Party to this Agreement, and, in any case, such amendment, waiver or consent shall be effective only in the specific instance and for the specific purpose for which given; provided that no amendment, waiver or consent that would adversely affect the rights of any Founder LLC Indemnitee hereunder shall be effective as to such Founder LLC Indemnitee without such Founder LLC Indemnitee’s prior written consent. The waiver by any Party of any breach of this Agreement shall not operate as or be construed to be a waiver by such Party of any subsequent breach.
Section 6.Assignment; Third-Party Beneficiaries. This Agreement and the rights of the Parties hereunder may not be assigned without the prior written consent of each of the Parties hereto. Subject to the foregoing, the provisions of this Agreement will be binding upon and inure to the benefit of the Parties hereto and their respective successors and assigns. The Company shall require any successor (whether direct or indirect, by purchase, merger, consolidation, conversion, division, operation of law or otherwise) to all or substantially all of

Section 9.Governing Law; Consent to Jurisdiction; Waiver of Jury Trial.
(a)Governing Law. This Agreement shall be interpreted, construed and governed by and in accordance with the internal law of the State of Delaware, without regard to any conflict of law principles thereof that otherwise would result in the application of the laws of another jurisdiction.
(b)Consent to Jurisdiction. In any claim, action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement, each of the Parties hereto (A) irrevocably and unconditionally consents and submits to the exclusive jurisdiction and venue of the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware; (B) agrees that it will not attempt to deny or defeat such jurisdiction by motion or other request for leave from such court; and (C) agrees that it will not bring any such action in any court other than the Court of Chancery of the State of Delaware, or, if (and only if) such court finds it lacks subject matter jurisdiction, another State court in Delaware or the Federal court of the United States of America sitting in Delaware, and appellate courts thereof. Service of process, summons, notice or document to any Party’s address and in the manner set forth in Section 7 shall be effective service of process for any such action.
(c)Waiver of Jury Trial. EACH PARTY HEREBY IRREVOCABLY AND UNCONDITIONALLY WAIVES ANY RIGHT TO A TRIAL BY JURY IN RESPECT OF ANY LITIGATION DIRECTLY OR INDIRECTLY ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT.
