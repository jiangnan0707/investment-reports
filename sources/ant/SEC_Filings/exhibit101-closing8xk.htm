STOCKHOLDERS’ AGREEMENT
OF
ANTHROPIC, PBC
This Stockholders’ Agreement (this “Agreement”) is effective as of the IPO Closing Date in connection with the Public Offering (as defined below) and is entered into by and among Anthropic, PBC, a Delaware public benefit corporation (the “Company”), Anthropic Founders, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the “Founder LLC”), and the Anthropic Long-Term Benefit Trust, a Delaware noncharitable purpose trust (the “Trust”). The Founder LLC and the Trust are collectively referred to herein as the “Stockholders.” Capitalized terms not otherwise defined herein shall have the meaning given to them in the Company’s Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation, duly adopted and filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Delaware in connection with the Public Offering as it may be amended or otherwise modified from time to time (the “Restated Certificate”).
RECITALS
WHEREAS, the Trust, as holder of all outstanding shares of the Class T Common Stock, has, and following the completion of the Public Offering will continue to have, certain governance rights with respect to the Company.
WHEREAS, in connection with the Public Offering, the Company has issued or will issue to the Founder LLC one (1) share of Class F Common Stock, which will grant certain governance rights with respect to the Company to the Founder LLC.
WHEREAS, the Restated Certificate provides that, at such times and subject to the conditions described therein, (i) the holder of the Class T Common Stock then outstanding, exclusively and as a separate class, shall be entitled to elect the smallest number of directors that constitutes a majority of the total number of authorized directors of the Company (collectively, the “Class T Directors” and each, a “Class T Director”) and (ii) the Electing Holders, exclusively and voting together as a single class, shall be entitled to elect the balance of the total number of authorized directors of the Company (collectively, the “At-Large Directors” and each, an “At-Large Director”); provided, however, that prior to the Class F Sunset Time, one Class T Director shall be designated by the holder of the Class T Common Stock then outstanding as the “Specified Class T Director” and subject to the other requirements set forth in the Restated Certificate.
WHEREAS, in connection with and to facilitate the completion of the Public Offering, the Company and the Stockholders desire to enter into this Agreement to ensure the governance rights of the parties.
NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:
1.Definitions. For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings specified below:
1.1“Board” means the Company’s Board of Directors.
1.2“Company Designated Official” has the meaning ascribed to it in the Founder LLC Agreement.


1.3“Founder LLC Agreement” means the Limited Liability Company Agreement of the Founder LLC, dated as of the IPO Closing Date, as amended or restated from time to time.
1.4“Member” means a member of the Founder LLC, as determined in accordance with the Founder LLC Agreement.
1.5“Necessary Action” means, with respect to a specified result, all actions necessary and appropriate to cause such result, including (i) calling special Board and stockholder meetings, (ii) voting or providing a written consent or proxy with respect to the applicable Shares, whether at any annual or special meeting, by written consent or otherwise, (iii) causing the adoption of stockholders’ resolutions and amendments to organizational documents of the Company or its subsidiaries, (iv) causing members of the Board to act in a certain manner or causing them to be removed in the event they do not act in such a manner, (v) executing agreements and instruments, (vi) making, or causing to be made, with governmental, administrative or regulatory authorities, all filings, registrations or similar actions that are required to achieve such result and (vii) nominating or appointing, or taking steps to cause the nomination or appointment of, certain persons (including to fill vacancies) and providing the highest level of support for the election or appointment of such persons to the Board or any committee thereof, including in connection with the annual or special meeting of the Company’s stockholders.
1.6“Nominating Committee” means a three-person committee of the Board, consisting of one Class T Director and two At-Large Directors, to which the Board has delegated the power and authority to (i) receive notice of nomination or removal of directors or a change in the Class T Director designated as the Specified Class T Director pursuant to this Agreement (or to waive or shorten any applicable notice periods), (ii) prior to the Class F Sunset Time, approve or reject a nomination or removal without cause of the Specified Class T Director or a change in the Class T Director designated as the Specified Class T Director, (iii) exercise any powers or rights of the NGC (as defined in the Founder LLC Agreement) pursuant to the Founder LLC Agreement, (iv) exercise any powers or rights of the Nominating Committee (as defined in the Trust Agreement) pursuant to the Trust Agreement and (v) exercise any powers or rights of the Nominating Committee pursuant to this Agreement, which as of the date hereof is the Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee of the Board (the powers and authorities referred to in clauses (i) through (v), the “Specified Authorities”).
1.7“Notification Date” means 30 calendar days prior to the date that the Company anticipates (i) filing a preliminary proxy statement or (ii) first mailing its proxy materials or a notice of availability of proxy materials (whichever is earlier) for a meeting of the Company’s stockholders. The Company will use reasonable best efforts to notify each of the Trust and the Founder LLC in writing of the date on which the Company anticipates (i) filing its preliminary proxy statement or (ii) first mailing its proxy materials or a notice of availability of proxy materials (whichever is earlier) no later than 40 calendar days prior to the first day on which a Notice (as defined below) would be required to be delivered.
1.8“Public Offering” means the Company’s initial public offering of the Class A Common Stock pursuant to an effective registration statement filed under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended.
1.9“Resource Agreement” means the Amended and Restated Resource and Information Agreement, dated as of the IPO Closing Date, by and among the Company and the Trust, as amended or restated from time to time.

2.Board Composition. Each Stockholder shall vote, or cause to be voted, all Shares owned by such Stockholder, or over which such Stockholder has voting control (to the extent such Shares are entitled to vote on such matters under the Restated Certificate or applicable law), from time to time and at all times, in whatever manner as shall be necessary and to take all Necessary Action to ensure that at each annual or special meeting of the Company’s stockholders at which an election of directors is held or the removal of any director is acted upon, or pursuant to any written consent of the stockholders, (x) the size and composition of the Board shall be set and remain as described in Sections 2.1 and 2.2 and (y) each nomination and removal of a director for which notice has been duly given, and (if applicable) which has been approved, in accordance with Section 2.5 shall be given effect, including as to the specific individual identified in any such notice of nomination.
2.1Board Size. Prior to the Class T Sunset Time, the total number of authorized directors of the Company that constitute the Whole Board shall be fixed solely by the Board acting pursuant to a resolution adopted by the affirmative vote of directors representing 80% of the Whole Board, rounded up or down to the nearest whole number (any exact 0.5 rounding up); provided that until the earlier to occur of the Class F Sunset Time or the Class T Sunset Time, the total number of authorized directors of the Company must be an odd number. From and after the Class T Sunset Time, the total number of authorized directors of the Company that constitute the Whole Board shall be fixed solely by resolution of the Board acting pursuant to a resolution adopted by the affirmative vote of directors representing a majority of the Whole Board.

(c)From and after the Class F Sunset Time and for so long as any shares of Class T Common Stock are outstanding, as the Class T Directors, a number of individuals, nominated by the holder of the Class T Common Stock then outstanding pursuant to Section 2.5(b), equal to the smallest

2.3Removal Rights. The parties will take all Necessary Action to ensure the removal without cause of any director as to which a Stockholder is entitled to nominating rights in Section 2.2 if such Stockholder provides notice to remove such director in compliance with the procedures set forth in Section 2.5.
2.4Vacancies and Newly Created Directorships. In the event of a vacancy or newly created directorship on the Board in any At-Large Director seat prior to the Class F Sunset Time, following receipt of a Notice from the Founder LLC in accordance with Section 2.5 and the Restated Certificate recommending an individual to fill such vacancy or newly created directorship, the Company and the Board shall take all Necessary Action to cause such individual to fill such vacancy or newly created directorship within 30 calendar days. Prior to the Class F Sunset Time, the Board will only fill vacancies or newly created directorships on the Board in any At-Large Director seat with the nominee named in any such Notice from the Founder LLC regarding such vacancy or newly created directorship. Solely for purposes of this Section 2.4, prior to the Class F Sunset Time, the Founder LLC may deliver a Notice in accordance with Section 2.5(b) at any time there is a vacancy or newly created directorship on the Board in any At-Large Director seat. For the avoidance of doubt, except as specifically provided in this Section 2.4 with respect to any action by the Board to fill a vacancy or newly created directorship in any At-Large Director seat prior to the Class F Sunset Time, vacancies or newly created directorships on the Board may be filled in any manner permitted by the Restated Certificate.
2.5Nomination and Removal Procedures.
(a)No Stockholder shall vote, or cause to be voted, its Shares (or any Shares over which such Stockholder has voting control (to the extent such Shares are entitled to vote on such matters under the Restated Certificate or applicable law)) for the election, appointment or removal of any director,

unless the nomination or removal of such director has been properly noticed and (if applicable) approved, in accordance with this Section 2.5.
(b)To nominate or remove a director without cause, a Stockholder (the “Acting Stockholder”) shall give written notice to the Nominating Committee and the other Stockholder at least 30 calendar days (in the case of a nomination or removal without cause of the Specified Class T Director, subject to Section 2.5(d)) prior to, as applicable, (i) in the case of a director to be elected, appointed or removed without cause at an annual or special meeting of the Company’s stockholders, the Notification Date for such annual or special meeting or (ii) in the case of a director to be elected, appointed or removed without cause by consent in lieu of meeting, the effectiveness of the stockholder action by consent in lieu of meeting electing, appointing or removing such director, in each case, identifying, in the case of a removal, the director to be removed, or in the case of a nomination, the person being nominated, appointed or elected, the director seat to be filled by such person, and unless waived by the Company or the Nominating Committee, the information required in Section 2.4(a)(iii)(1) of the Bylaws (each, a “Notice”). The Acting Stockholder shall promptly provide, or use commercially reasonable efforts to cause the nominated individual to provide, the Nominating Committee with such additional information regarding the nominee as the Nominating Committee may reasonably request.
(c)To remove a director for cause, the Acting Stockholder shall give written notice to the Nominating Committee and the other Stockholder at least 30 calendar days prior to, as applicable, (i) in the case of a director to be removed for cause at an annual or special meeting of the Company’s stockholders, the Notification Date for such annual or special meeting or (ii) in the case of a director to be removed for cause by consent in lieu of meeting, the effectiveness of the stockholder action by consent in lieu of meeting removing such director, in each case, identifying the director to be removed and providing a reasonably detailed description of the grounds for such removal for cause.
(d)With respect to the Specified Class T Director:
(i)    No individual may be nominated to serve as the Specified Class T Director and the then-serving Specified Class T Director may not be removed without cause unless such nomination or removal, as the case may be, has first been approved by action by a majority of the authorized number of members of the Nominating Committee in accordance with Section 2.5(d)(ii) below; provided, however, that, in the event of an NC No Vote (as defined below), such nomination or removal shall instead require the prior approval of the Requisite Class E Vote; provided further that, in the event of an NC No Vote, if the Requisite Class E Vote is not obtained with respect to such nomination or removal, such individual shall not be nominated, elected, appointed or removed, as applicable, as the Specified Class T Director; provided, further, that if no Class E Common Stock is then outstanding, the determination of the Nominating Committee will be final. For the avoidance of doubt, from and after the Class F Sunset Time, any Specified Class T Director then serving may be nominated, elected or removed by the holder of the Class T Common Stock then outstanding without compliance with this Section 2.5(d). Notwithstanding anything herein to the contrary, if the Nominating Committee has not received a Notice of nomination or removal with respect to a sitting Specified Class T Director on or prior to the Notification Date for the annual meeting of the Company’s stockholders at which such Specified Class T Director’s term of office ends, then the holder of Class T Common Stock shall be deemed to have provided Notice nominating such director for re-election pursuant to this Section 2.5.
(ii)    The Nominating Committee will as soon as reasonably practicable following receipt of a Notice relating to the removal without cause of, or the nomination of a person to serve as, a Specified Class T Director consider the Notice in good faith and notify each of the

Trust and the Founder LLC of the Nominating Committee’s decision to approve or deny such nomination or removal; provided that, if the Nominating Committee fails to approve such nomination or removal within fifteen (15) calendar days of receipt of the Notice, then such failure to approve will be deemed a denial of such nomination or removal (any denial or such failure to approve, an “NC No Vote”). In the event of an NC No Vote, the Trust may either give written notice to the Company that the Trust desires to (A) submit such nomination or removal of the Specified Class T Director to a vote of the holders of Class E Common Stock or (B) abandon such proposed nomination or removal (the “Class E Notice”). (f)Each Stockholder may nominate individuals to any director seat that such Stockholder is entitled to nominate pursuant to Section 2.2 only in accordance with Sections 2.4 and 2.5, and will not utilize any other stockholder nomination procedures, including pursuant to the Bylaws.

If the Bilateral Parties deliver a Bilateral Request, the Company shall take all Necessary Action to give effect to the Bilateral Request.

3.6Class T Notice Rights. At any time when any shares of Class T Common Stock are outstanding, the Company shall not, either directly or indirectly by amendment, merger, consolidation, domestication, transfer, continuance, waiver, statutory conversion, recapitalization, reclassification or otherwise, in one or more transactions or a series of related transactions, do any of the following without providing reasonable notice in writing to the Trust at least five (5) calendar days prior to taking such action or authorizing such transaction, and any such act or transaction authorized without providing such notice shall be null and void, and of no force or effect unless such notice is waived in writing by the Trust either before or after such action is taken or such transaction is authorized; provided, however, the Trust shall be deemed to have waived such notice if the Trust does not object to such act or transaction in writing within five (5) calendar days of the time that the Trust becomes aware of such act or transaction:
(a)(i) effect a Liquidation Event, (ii) transfer control of an AI model exceeding capabilities thresholds to be established by the Board, and re-established by the Board on a quarterly basis thereafter or (iii) effect the sale or other transfer of capital stock of the Company representing 50% or more of (A) the voting power of the then-outstanding capital stock of the Company entitled to vote generally or (B) the economic interest represented by all of the then-outstanding capital stock of the Company, or consent to any of the foregoing actions set forth in clauses (i)-(iii);
(b) (i) apply for or consent to the appointment of a receiver, trustee, custodian, or liquidator of the Company or all or substantially all of its property, (ii) make an assignment for the benefit of creditors, (iii) institute any proceedings under the United States Bankruptcy Code or any other federal or state bankruptcy, reorganization, receivership, insolvency, or other similar law affecting the rights of creditors generally, (iv) file a petition or answer seeking reorganization or an arrangement with creditors to take advantage of any insolvency law or (v) file an answer admitting the material allegations of a bankruptcy, reorganization, or insolvency petition filed against the Company; or
(c)except in connection with any intercompany agreement or transaction or internal reorganization or restructuring, permit any direct or indirect subsidiary of the Company to take, or agree to take, any of the actions in Section 3.6(a)-(b) (with references therein to the “Company” being deemed to mean such direct or indirect subsidiary of the Company) in a single transaction or series of related transactions.

3.9Expenses; Advancement. The Company shall advance to each of the Founder LLC and the Trust, or pay directly, all reasonable and documented out-of-pocket obligations, costs, fees and expenses, including the reasonable fees and expenses of counsel to each such party (collectively, “Expenses”), incurred in connection with (i) the administration and maintenance of such party, including tax returns and filings (including, but not limited to, FBAR filings and other regulatory filings); (ii) the ownership, voting or disposition of the Shares, including the appraisal thereof; (iii) the exercise or performance of, or the failure to exercise or perform, any right, power, duty or obligation under this Agreement, the Restated Certificate, the Bylaws, the Founder LLC Agreement, the Trust Agreement or the Resource Agreement; or (iv) the dissolution, winding up or termination of, or any post-termination liability in respect of, the Founder LLC or the Trust (each of clauses (i) through (iv), a “Covered Matter”), in each case promptly following a written request therefor. To the extent any such expense has already been paid by such party, the Company shall instead reimburse such party promptly following a written request therefor. Neither the Founder LLC nor the Trust shall be required to incur any liability, or to advance any funds, in respect of any Covered Matter other than from amounts provided by the Company, and the rights of each such party under this Section 3.9 are not conditioned upon the execution or delivery of any indemnification agreement contemplated by Section 3.10.
