ANTHROPIC, PBC
A DELAWARE PUBLIC BENEFIT CORPORATION
Anthropic, PBC, a public benefit corporation organized and existing under and by virtue of the provisions of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware (the “Delaware General Corporation Law”), 
DOES HEREBY CERTIFY:
FIRST: That the name of this corporation is Anthropic, PBC and that this corporation was originally incorporated pursuant to the Delaware General Corporation Law on January 26, 2021.
SECOND: That this Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation of this corporation was duly adopted in accordance with Sections 242 and 245 of the Delaware General Corporation Law and has been duly approved by the written consent of the stockholders of this corporation in accordance with Section 228 of the Delaware General Corporation Law. 
THIRD: That the Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation of this corporation be amended and restated in its entirety as follows:
ARTICLE I
The name of this corporation is Anthropic, PBC (the “Corporation”).
ARTICLE II
The address of the registered office of the Corporation in the State of Delaware is 251 Little Falls Drive, in the City of Wilmington, New Castle County, Delaware 19808. The name of its registered agent at such address is Corporation Service Company.
ARTICLE III
The specific public benefit that the Corporation will promote is to responsibly develop and maintain advanced AI for the long-term benefit of humanity. In addition, the Corporation may engage in any lawful act or activity for which corporations may be organized under the Delaware General Corporation Law.
ARTICLE IV
The Corporation is authorized to issue five classes of stock to be designated, respectively, “Participating Common Stock,” “Class E Common Stock,” “Class F Common Stock,” “Class T Common Stock” and “Preferred Stock.” The Class E Common Stock, Class F Common Stock and Class T Common Stock are collectively referred to as the “Nonparticipating Common Stock.” The total number of shares of Participating Common Stock authorized to be issued is 400,000,000,000 shares, par value $0.00001 per share, of which 300,000,000,000 shares are designated as a series of Participating


Common Stock denominated as Class A Common Stock (the “Class A Common Stock”), and of which 100,000,000,000 shares are designated as a series of Participating Common Stock denominated as Class N Common Stock (the “Class N Common Stock”). The total number of shares of Class E Common Stock authorized to be issued is 30,000,000,000 shares, par value $0.00001 per share. The total number of shares of Class F Common Stock authorized to be issued is 1 share, par value $0.00001 per share. The total number of shares of Class T Common Stock authorized to be issued is 1 share, par value $0.00001 per share. The total number of shares of Preferred Stock authorized to be issued is 30,000,000,000 shares, par value $0.00001 per share.

5.2Rights of Participating Common Stock.
(a)Identical Rights. Except as otherwise provided in this Amended and Restated Certificate or required by applicable law, shares of Participating Common Stock shall have the same rights and powers, rank equally (including as to dividends and distributions, and any liquidation, dissolution or winding up of the Corporation but excluding voting and other matters as described in ARTICLE V, Section 5.6 below), share ratably and be identical in all respects as to all matters, including:
(i)Subject to the prior rights of holders of all classes and series of stock at the time outstanding having prior rights as to dividends, the holders of Participating Common Stock shall be entitled to receive, when, as and if declared by the Board, out of any assets of the Corporation legally available therefor, such dividends as may be declared from time to time by the Board. Any dividends paid to the holders of shares of Participating Common Stock shall be paid pro rata, on an equal priority, pari passu basis, unless different treatment of the shares of any such series is approved by the affirmative vote of the holders of a majority of the voting power of the outstanding shares of such applicable series of Participating Common Stock treated adversely, voting separately as a series.
(ii)The Corporation shall not declare or pay any dividend or make any other distribution to the holders of Participating Common Stock payable in securities of the Corporation unless the same dividend or distribution with the same record date and payment date shall be declared and paid on all shares of Participating Common Stock; provided, however, that (i) dividends or other distributions payable in shares of Class A Common Stock or rights to acquire shares of Class A Common Stock may be declared and paid to the holders of Class A Common Stock without the same dividend or distribution being declared and paid to the holders of the Class N Common Stock if, and only if, a dividend payable in shares of Class N Common Stock or rights to acquire shares of Class N Common Stock is declared and

paid to the holders of Class N Common Stock at the same rate and with the same record date and payment date; and (ii) dividends or other distributions payable in shares of Class N Common Stock or rights to acquire shares of Class N Common Stock may be declared and paid to the holders of Class N Common Stock without the same dividend or distribution being declared and paid to the holders of the Class A Common Stock if, and only if, a dividend payable in shares of Class A Common Stock or rights to acquire shares of Class A Common Stock is declared and paid to the holders of Class A Common Stock at the same rate and with the same record date and payment date; and provided, further, that nothing in the foregoing shall prevent the Corporation from declaring and paying dividends or other distributions payable in shares of one series of Participating Common Stock or rights to acquire one series of Participating Common Stock to holders of the other series of Participating Common Stock, or, with the approval of holders of a majority of the outstanding shares of each of the Class A Common Stock and Class N Common Stock, each voting separately as a series, from providing for different treatment of the shares of Class A Common Stock and Class N Common Stock in connection with any dividends or distributions.
(iii)If the Corporation in any manner subdivides or combines the outstanding shares of Class A Common Stock or Class N Common Stock, then the outstanding shares of all Participating Common Stock will be subdivided or combined in the same proportion and manner, unless different treatment of the shares of Class A Common Stock and Class N Common Stock is approved by the affirmative vote of the holders of a majority of the outstanding shares of each of the Class A Common Stock and Class N Common Stock, each voting separately as a series.
(b)Class N Common Stock Conversion.
(i)Automatic Conversion upon a Class N Transfer. (ii)Delivery of Shares Upon Conversion. As soon as reasonably practicable after the Conversion Time, the Corporation shall use its reasonable best efforts to deliver, or cause to be delivered, the shares of Class A Common Stock issuable upon conversion of Class N Common Stock (i) to the transferee in a Market Transfer in any manner permitted by the Depository Trust Company or another established clearing corporation or securities exchange performing similar functions or (ii) to the transferee in a Private Transfer in any manner permitted by the Delaware General Corporation Law and the Bylaws of the Corporation.
(iii)Procedures. The Corporation may, from time to time, establish reasonable policies and procedures relating to the conversion of Class N Common Stock to Class A Common Stock and the general administration of this stock structure, including the issuance of stock certificates with respect thereto, in its reasonable discretion. As a condition to any Market Transfer or Private Transfer, the Corporation may require reasonable certifications, affidavits or other proof to verify that such transfer qualifies as a Market Transfer or Private Transfer. Without limiting the foregoing, for purposes of determining whether a transfer constitutes or would constitute a Market Transfer or Private Transfer, the Corporation may require, among other things, written representations from (i) the transferor, (ii) the transferor’s bank, broker, financial intermediary, or other nominee, (iii) in the case of a Private Transfer, the purchaser or acquiror, and/or (iv) in the case of a Private Transfer, the purchaser’s or acquiror’s bank, broker or other nominee. The Corporation shall be entitled, in its sole discretion, to

(iv)Immediate Effect. In the event of and upon a conversion of shares of Class N Common Stock to shares of Class A Common Stock pursuant to ARTICLE V, Section 5.2(b), such conversion shall be deemed to have been made as of the applicable Conversion Time. Upon any conversion of Class N Common Stock to Class A Common Stock in accordance with this Amended and Restated Certificate, all rights of the holder of such shares of Class N Common Stock shall cease and the person or persons in whose name or names the certificate or certificates representing the shares of Class A Common Stock are to be issued shall be treated for all purposes as having become the record holder or holders of such shares of Class A Common Stock.

The issuance of uncertificated shares for Class A Common Stock shall be made without charge to any Class N Holder for any documentary stamp or similar taxes that may be payable in respect of the issue or delivery of such uncertificated shares, provided that the Corporation shall not be required to pay any tax that may be payable in respect of any transfer involved in the issuance and delivery of any such uncertificated shares upon conversion and the Corporation shall not be required to issue or deliver such uncertificated shares unless or until the person or persons requesting the issuance thereof shall have paid to the Corporation the amount of such tax or shall have reasonably established to the Corporation that such tax has been paid.

(a)Dividend Rights. The holders of shares of Class E Common Stock, as such, shall not be entitled to receive dividends.
(b)Redemption.
(i)Upon Class F Sunset Time. Each share of Class E Common Stock shall be automatically redeemed by the Corporation without consideration and deemed automatically forfeited by the holder thereof upon the Class F Sunset Time.
(ii)Upon Death, Incapacity, or Cessation of Service for Cause or Service to a Direct Competitor. Each share of Class E Common Stock held by a holder of Class E Common Stock shall be automatically redeemed by the Corporation without consideration and deemed automatically forfeited by such holder upon (A) such holder’s death or Incapacity, (B) such holder’s ceasing to be a Service Provider for Cause or (C) a determination by the Corporation that such holder has commenced employment or other service with a Direct Competitor.

(iii)Following Cessation of Service Other Than for Cause. Except as otherwise provided in ARTICLE V, Sections 5.3(b)(ii) or 5.3(b)(iv), one half of the shares of Class E Common Stock then held by a holder of Class E Common Stock (rounded up to the nearest whole share) shall be automatically redeemed by the Corporation without consideration and deemed automatically forfeited by such holder on each anniversary of such holder’s ceasing to be a Service Provider for any reason other than for Cause.
(iv)Redemption of All Class E Common Stock. Each share of Class E Common Stock shall be automatically redeemed by the Corporation without consideration and deemed automatically forfeited by the holder thereof upon the date and time or the happening of the event that is specified by (A) the affirmative vote of directors representing 80% of the Whole Board, rounded up or down to the nearest whole number (any exact 0.5 rounding up), (B) the holder of the Class F Common Stock then outstanding and (C) the holder of the Class T Common Stock then outstanding.

The Corporation shall be entitled, in its sole discretion, to accept without further investigation the accuracy of any such certifications, affidavits or other proof as is provided. The Corporation’s determination of whether grounds for the redemption of Class E Common Stock exist shall be conclusive and binding to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law. No delay in the redemption of Class E Common Stock as permitted hereunder will have the effect of waiving the Corporation’s right to redeem such Class E Common Stock; provided that for purposes of the determination of holders of Class E Common Stock entitled to vote with respect to a particular matter, the Corporation’s records shall be conclusive and binding to the fullest extent permitted by applicable law.
