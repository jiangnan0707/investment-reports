Item 3.02 Unregistered Sales of Equity Securities.
On November 20, 2026, immediately subsequent to the closing of the IPO, the Company completed the private placement of an aggregate of 313,253,007 shares of its Class N common stock, par value $0.00001 per share (the “Class N Common Stock”), to Advanced Micro Devices, Inc., Broadcom Inc., Google LLC, Micron Technology, Inc., Microsoft Corporation, NVIDIA Corporation, and TSMC Partners, Ltd. (collectively, the “Purchasers”), severally and not jointly, at a price per share equal to the IPO Price (as defined below) (the “Concurrent Private Placement”). The aggregate proceeds from the Concurrent Private Placement were approximately $26.0 billion. No underwriting discounts or commissions were paid with respect to the Concurrent Private Placement. The Concurrent Private Placement was conducted as a non-public transaction and, as a transaction by an issuer not involving a public offering, is exempt from registration in reliance upon Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act. The shares of Class N Common Stock sold in the Concurrent Private Placement have not been registered under the Securities Act, are “restricted securities” under the Securities Act, and may be resold only pursuant to an effective registration statement or an available exemption from registration.
The Class N Common Stock has no voting rights, except as otherwise required by law. Under the Company’s amended and restated certificate of incorporation (the “Restated Certificate”), each share of Class N Common Stock will convert into one share of Class A Common Stock upon (i) a transfer effected through a registered offering or an open market sale of such shares or (ii) a transfer to a third party (A) that is not a holder of Class N Common Stock


(or an affiliate of a holder of Class N Common Stock) and (B) that will not beneficially own or control more than 9.99% of the shares of any class or series of the Company’s equity securities after giving effect to the transfer.
On November 20, 2026, immediately prior to the closing of the IPO, the Company completed the issuance and sale of one share of its Class F common stock, par value $0.00001 per share (the “Class F Common Stock”), to the Founder LLC for a purchase price of $7.00 (the “Class F Issuance”). No underwriting discounts or commissions were paid with respect to the Class F Issuance. The Class F Issuance was conducted as a non-public transaction and, as a transaction by an issuer not involving a public offering, is exempt from registration in reliance upon Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act. The share of Class F Common Stock sold in the Class F Issuance has not been registered under the Securities Act and is a “restricted security” under the Securities Act.
The Class F Common Stock is entitled to an aggregate number of votes that equals 50.1% of the total voting power of the Company’s outstanding shares of capital stock as of the applicable record date on matters submitted to a vote of stockholders generally, including the election of directors other than the Company’s Class T directors. The Class F Common Stock is not convertible into any other class or series of capital stock.
